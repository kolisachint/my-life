# Housing: the rent solve

_2026-08-27. Supersedes the "just stay" conclusion in `where-to-live.md` — renting
changes the maths that buying could not._

## Recommendation

**Let out D303 Chikhali. Rent a 2BHK in Wakad. Transfer both children to SNBP
Rahatani for the April 2027 academic year.**

Net cost ≈ **₹6,000/month**. Commute 25 km → 7 km. School run unchanged at ~5 km.
No stamp duty. Reversible on an 11-month lease.

## The unlock: SNBP is a group, not one school

Every earlier version of this died on the same rock — home is well placed for
school, badly placed for work, and any move toward Hinjawadi abandons SNBP Moshi.

SNBP runs campuses at **Rahatani** (Survey No. 29/2, off Rahatani–Pimple Saudagar
Rd, near Akashganga Society, 411017) and Pimpri Colony as well as Moshi.

- Rahatani → Wakad ≈ 5 km
- Rahatani → Hinjawadi ≈ 7 km

So Wakad keeps the school run at roughly today's distance, and it is an
**intra-group transfer**, not a school change.

**Verify first — the plan rests on it:** does SNBP Rahatani have seats in both
grades for the April 2027 intake, and does an internal transfer get preference?
If no, stop and stay in Chikhali.

## Shortlist (verify rent and availability — listings move)

- **Wakad** — Akshar Elementa; older societies off Datta Mandir Rd / Kaspate Wasti
  rent below the new towers. 2BHK ₹18–25k.
- **Tathawade** — Rama Metro Life Ultima, Garve 33 Central Avenue, Mahindra
  Happinest, Yashada Evo. ~9 km to work.
- **Punawale** — Pharande Puneville (28-acre), Supreme Riverside, iRise Punawale.
  2BHK ~₹23,400, 3BHK ~₹30,200. Further from Rahatani.

**Before signing:** visit on a weekday 6–8pm, drive to Sahyadri Park at 9am on a
Tuesday, and check water supply (tanker dependence is common here and never
appears in listings).

**The deposit bites:** Pune deposits run 3–6 months' rent. ₹1.1–1.85 lakh needed
in hand by February — five to nine months of saving at ₹20k/month. Refundable,
unlike stamp duty, but ring-fence it now.

## Caveats

Rents and society names come from market listings and change — verify. Distances
approximate; drive them. D303 rental income is taxable and a live loan changes the
interest treatment — put it to the CA alongside the open tax questions. The Pink
Line metro serves Balewadi/Baner, not PCMC north; do not count on it.
