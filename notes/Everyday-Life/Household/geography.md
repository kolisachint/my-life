## Commute

Chikhali → Hinjawadi Phase 3: **~25 km, 60–90 min each way** depending on time of
day. Hinjawadi's congestion is structural, not seasonal — treat 90 min as the
planning number for a morning arrival.

This is why the recurring **"Raise WFO exception"** task (every 21st) is one of the
highest-value items in the whole system. It is not admin — it is worth roughly a
month of life per year.

## Jurisdiction: PCMC, not PMC

Chikhali and Moshi fall under **Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation**, not Pune
Municipal Corporation. This changes where several recurring tasks are actioned:

- **Property tax** for D303 Anjani Gaatha → PCMC portal, not PMC.
- **Birth certificates** (both children) → PCMC e-services. Records are digitised,
  so a certified copy is usually downloadable online. An affidavit is required
  only if the registration was made more than a year after the birth.
- Ward-office matters → PCMC ward covering Chikhali.

Batch these: one PCMC portal session covers property tax and any certificate.

## Passport

**POPSK Pimpri-Chinchwad** — opposite Hindustan Antibiotics Factory, Kharalwadi,
Old Pune–Mumbai Highway, Pune 411018. Roughly 10–12 km / 30 min from Chikhali.

Confirm the centre actually assigned to PIN 411062 on passportindia.gov.in at
booking time; the above is from a third-party listing, and the portal assigns by
PIN.

Because the centre is near **home** and far from **work**, a morning slot
(09:00–11:00) means being done by noon and working the afternoon from home —
rather than losing a full day. Prefer morning slots.

Sequence on Passport Seva is **fill form → pay → then book the slot.** The
appointment cannot be booked before payment.
