## What it fixes, and what it does not

**Fixed.** Three of the five resume defects recorded on 2026-08-27 are gone in
this document: TCS is present, Sears is correctly closed at 2021, and the tenure
reads 18 years rather than 14.

**Not fixed — and this is the point.** A TCS profile card is not a market
instrument. It is written for a client buying a TCS engagement, so it:

1. **Names clients, not employers,** and gives no continuous employment history —
   the first thing a recruiter screens for.
2. **Carries TCS branding and a "Classification: Public" stamp.** It cannot be
   sent to a hiring manager as-is.
3. Still has **no AI/ML/GenAI content**, despite the Rust embedding library and
   the semantic-search work. That was defect #4 and it stands.
4. Leads a certifications block that is otherwise **20-year-old technology**
   (Oracle, Teradata).
5. Quantifies exactly **one** achievement ($600K). Everything else is
   responsibility language — "architected", "delivered", "led" — with no numbers.

So the instrument problem is **half solved**. The raw material now exists and is
current; it has not been assembled into a document he can send.
