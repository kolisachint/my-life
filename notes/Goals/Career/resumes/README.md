**Why 4 and 5** (you left two open). An ATS version is not a style choice — the
portals that reject you never show a human your file, and a two-column PDF with
tables parses to garbage. A referral one-pager is the other end: `job-options.md`
concluded senior roles move by referral, and what a referrer actually needs is
one page they can forward without editing.

## The confidentiality split

Client names are the whole question, and the answer is different per document:

- **Public surfaces** (LinkedIn, personal site) — no client names. Lloyds,
  Barclays, Saudi Telecom, Sears, Cisco and Telstra all reached you through a
  services contract; naming them on a public profile is a real contractual and
  reputational risk. They become "a UK tier-1 retail bank", "a Gulf national
  telecom operator". **You lose nothing** — the scale descriptor carries the
  weight, and every recruiter in this market reads the code fluently.
- **Private, addressed documents** (a resume you send to a named recruiter, an
  ATS submission, a referral one-pager) — name them. This is standard practice
  for services professionals and is what buyers expect.
