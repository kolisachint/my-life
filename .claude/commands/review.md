---
description: Run the weekly review ritual
---

Run `make review`. Then work through, in order:

1. **Triage overdue.** For each: do now, reschedule with a real date, or delete.
   Never leave one overdue with no decision.
2. **Stalled goals.** Find goals with no dated child action. Pick at most two and
   attach one concrete dated action each. Do not try to fix all of them.
3. **The week ahead.** Name the two things that actually matter, and say out loud
   that everything else is optional.
4. **Neglect check.** Anything that should move to ⛔️ Neglect?

Apply changes with the `td` CLI. Confirm before any delete. Then append to
`memory/learned.txt` and `state/log.md`, and commit.
