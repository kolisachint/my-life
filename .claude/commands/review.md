---
description: Run the weekly review ritual
---

Run `make review`. Then work through, in order:

1. **Triage overdue.** For each, decide: do now, reschedule with a real date, or
   delete. Never leave an item overdue with no decision.
2. **Stalled goals.** The brief lists goals with no dated next action. Pick at
   most two and attach one concrete dated action each. Do not try to fix all of them.
3. **The week ahead.** Name the two things that actually matter. Everything else
   is optional and should be said out loud to be optional.
4. **Neglect check.** Is anything on the list that should move to ⛔️ Neglect?

Apply changes with `./bin/todoist` — confirm with Sachin before any delete.
Then append a dated entry to `state/log.md` and commit.
