The architecture (Goals / BucketList / Everyday Life, split by section) is good and stays.

1. **Every goal gets exactly one dated next action — or it moves to Neglect.** No third option.
2. **Fix the PCA task.** Replace with *"PCA study — 45 minutes"*, every workday, plus a **booked
   exam date**. Nothing makes a study habit real like a paid, non-refundable slot.
3. **Collapse five rituals into two + one quarterly.** Keep Weekly Review (Sun) and Monthly Money

   stays — it's four conversations a year, not a loop.
4. **Automate the transfers.** Your own goal, actionless since Oct 2023, and the highest-leverage
   item in the system. One SIP mandate on the 2nd, right after salary credit — then the savings
   goal survives your worst weeks.
5. **Give Tayadi's ₹2 lakh a date and an outcome.** Recovered, scheduled, or written off — all
   three are fine. Ambiguity isn't.
6. **Move the engineering side-projects out of Everyday Life.** The Rust embedding library and
   the hoo-code enhancements are serious technical specs sitting beside "buy dash camera". Either
   a real project with weekly time, or Neglect. Right now they're neither, so they're stress.
7. **Sync the yearly-finance parent with its children.** Parent is dated Aug 2027 while ITR,
   property tax and PPF are all due this week. Re-date the parent to 1 July so it lands *before*
   filing season.
